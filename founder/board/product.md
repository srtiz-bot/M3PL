# Product lens
Score: 5 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The price is the clearest thing in the business: $149 a month plus $1.95 an order, a calculator, carrier at cost. A buyer can understand it in one read. That is rare and worth protecting.
- The first step is low effort: 14-day trial, Marcana does the setup and store connections. The customer does not have to do the hard part.
- One warehouse, one warehouse system (ShipHero), one client portal. Few seams on the operations side.
- Bilingual support is a real experience difference for Spanish-speaking sellers, not a slogan.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No one thing.** Six service pages, four customer stages, volume from under 100 to 5,000+ a month, four industries. A brand-new seller and a 5,000-order brand need different products. Serving both from one small warehouse means doing neither brilliantly. The pitch currently needs a paragraph.
2. **The promise outruns the proof.** "Same-day shipping" and "no hidden fees" are the experience, yet no order cutoff time is stated, hours are weekdays 9:30 to 5:30, and storage, receiving and returns pricing are not published. The first surprise invoice or the first Friday-evening order that ships Monday breaks the promise. Accuracy, on-time rate and capacity are all unknown.
3. **The seams.** Lead comes in through form, call, text, WhatsApp or Instagram; then onboarding; then inbound receiving; then daily picking; then returns; then billing. Each handoff is between people or systems, and team size is unknown. Reply "within one business day" is slow for a seller choosing a warehouse today.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence, who is this for and what do you do better than anyone for them?
- Which of the six services brings the most orders and the fewest problems? Which would you cut?
- What is the cutoff time for same-day shipping, and what share of orders actually ship same day?
- What does a client see in the first five minutes after signing up? In the first week, when inventory arrives?
- What is the pick error rate, and how does a client find out about a mistake: from you or from their customer?
- What does a client pay that is not on the pricing page (storage, receiving, returns, special projects)?
- How many trials become paying clients, and why do the others leave?
- How many clients and orders can this warehouse handle before quality drops?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Pick one customer and one lead service, and say it in one sentence on the home page. Keep the others as add-ons, not equal headlines.
2. Publish every fee, or state plainly that there are none, including storage, receiving and returns.
3. State the same-day cutoff time and report the real same-day rate and error rate monthly.
4. Write down the first-14-days experience step by step, with one named owner per handoff.
5. Supply the missing numbers: clients, monthly orders, trial conversion, churn, capacity.
