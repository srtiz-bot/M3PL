# Board brief: Marcana 3PL

This is an existing, operating business being reviewed, not a pre-launch idea.
Everything below comes from the company's own public website. Nothing has been
independently verified.

## The business

Marcana 3PL is a third-party logistics (3PL) and e-commerce fulfillment company
in Miami, Florida. It receives and stores online sellers' inventory, picks and
packs their orders, and ships them the same day, across the US and
internationally. It is founder-led by Saul Ortiz, described as an e-commerce
founder with 10+ years of B2C and B2B fulfillment experience. Support is
bilingual, English and Spanish.

## Who it is for

Small and growing e-commerce brands. The quote form sorts leads into four
stages: "Startup" (still setting up), "Launch" (some orders coming in),
"Viability" (consistent daily orders) and "Scaling", and into volume bands from
under 100 to 5,000+ shipments a month. Industries named: apparel, vitamins and
supplements, cosmetics, consumable goods.

## What it sells

Six service pages, all delivered from one Miami warehouse:

- E-commerce store fulfillment (Shopify, WooCommerce, Etsy)
- TikTok Shop order fulfillment
- Amazon FBA prep (inspection, FNSKU labels, poly bagging, bundling, shipping to Amazon)
- Amazon FBM fulfillment
- FedEx One Rate fulfillment (flat-rate packaging, 2-3 day nationwide delivery)
- Inventory management on the ShipHero warehouse system, with a client portal

Returns handling, quality checks and restocking are also offered.

## Price

One published plan, the "Growth Plan":

- $149 per month
- plus $1.95 per order shipped, covering up to 2 lbs and 2 items per order
- extra item picks estimated at $0.35 each
- carrier shipping billed separately, at cost
- includes a warehouse address, portal access, pick, pack and same-day shipping
- marketed as "no hidden fees"
- no storage fee, receiving fee or setup fee is published
- 14-day free trial: fulfillment fees waived, client pays carrier cost only
- Marcana handles implementation and store connections
- custom quotes for higher volume

Example from the site's calculator: 500 orders a month comes to $1,124, or
$2.25 per order, before carrier cost.

## How customers find it

A bilingual website (marcana-3pl.com) with a separate SEO page per service in
English and Spanish, a pricing calculator, a quote request form, a
book-a-call link, phone/text, WhatsApp, and Instagram. The site promises a reply
within one business day.

## Where

One location: 19599 NE 10th Ave, Suite A-B, Miami, FL 33179. Hours Monday to
Friday, 9:30am to 5:30pm ET.

## Not known

Current number of clients, monthly order volume, revenue, cost per order, rent,
warehouse size and capacity, team size, how many leads the site produces, what
it costs to win a client, client churn, and who the direct competitors are.
