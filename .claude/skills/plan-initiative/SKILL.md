# Plan an initiative

1. **Frame it.** Restate the idea as a goal with a number and a date, name the brand, and ask the
   owner up to three questions if budget, timing or success measure is unclear.
2. **Brief in parallel** only the leads it touches:
   - Strategy: is this worth doing, against what alternatives, and what would make it fail?
   - Marketing: channel plan, messages, assets needed, expected cost and return.
   - Ops: inventory, fulfillment and support impact; lead times.
   - Finance: cost, cash timing, margin and break-even.
3. **Reconcile.** Find conflicts (marketing wants to launch before stock lands; the budget doesn't
   cover the plan) and resolve them or put the trade-off to the owner.
4. **Write the plan** to `org/drafts/<brand>/plan-<short-name>.md`:
   - Goal and success measure
   - Recommendation and the main alternative considered
   - Milestones with dates and the owner agent for each
   - Budget and expected return
   - Risks and what we'd watch
   - Actions needing approval (handled through the approvals skill)
5. Once approved, add the goal to `org/goals.md` and log the decision.
