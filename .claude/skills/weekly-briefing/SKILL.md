# Weekly briefing

Run by the Chief of Staff. Covers the last 7 days against the 7 days before.

1. Read `org/goals.md`, every file in `org/brands/`, `org/accounts.md`, and the last entries in
   `org/decisions-log.md`.
2. Brief the four leads in parallel, one brief per lead covering every brand that has
   data for that department. Ask each for: the three numbers that matter most, what
   changed and why, the top risk, and the top proposed action.
3. Check the reports, then write one briefing:
   - **Needs your decision**: at most five items, each with a recommendation.
   - **Wax brands** (Starpil, Nova, Wax Supply): two or three lines each, then one line comparing
     them, since they share customers and compete for the same budget.
   - **Services** (Marcana 3PL, Vende en USA): two or three lines each.
   - **Progress against goals.**
   - **Gaps**: data that could not be read, and which connector would fix it.
4. Keep it to one page. Detail stays in the leads' reports, available on request.
5. Execute nothing. Proposed actions wait for the owner's approval.
