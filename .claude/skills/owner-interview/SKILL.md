# Owner interview

Goal: replace "to confirm" with facts the owner states, in short sessions.

1. Scan `org/brands/*.md`, `org/accounts.md` and `org/goals.md` for "to confirm" and empty sections.
2. Prioritize: goals first, then whatever blocks the work the owner asked for, then the rest.
3. Ask at most five questions per round, grouped by brand, using multiple-choice where you can
   offer sensible options. Prefill answers you can infer from connectors or websites and ask the
   owner to confirm or correct them, rather than asking from scratch.
4. Write each confirmed answer into the right file straight away, and log a one-line entry in
   `org/decisions-log.md` for goals and any policy (pricing, discount limits, things never to say).
5. Stop after one round and ask whether to continue. Never invent an answer the owner didn't give.

Goals need a number and a date: "Grow Nova revenue" is not a goal; "Nova online revenue
$X/month by March 31" is.

Questions worth covering per brand: who the ideal customer is; the top products to push; how the
brand differs from the sister brands; voice (three words plus a phrase it would never use);
discount and claim limits; active ad, social and email accounts; who on the team handles what.
