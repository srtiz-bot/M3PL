# Monthly review

Covers the last full calendar month against the month before and, where data exists, the same
month last year.

1. Brief all four leads in parallel, one brief each covering every brand they have data for:
   - Marketing: spend, revenue attributed, cost per acquisition, best and worst channel.
   - Ops: orders shipped, late or problem orders, stockouts, support volume and satisfaction.
   - Finance: revenue, gross margin, cash position, overdue receivables, unusual costs.
   - Strategy: what changed in the market or with competitors that matters.
2. Score each goal in `org/goals.md`: on track, at risk, or off track, with the number.
3. Write the review to `org/drafts/cross-brand/monthly-review-YYYY-MM.md`:
   - Headline: the three things that mattered most
   - Per brand: results vs goals, one paragraph
   - What to stop, start and continue next month
   - Proposed goal changes, for the owner to approve
4. Ask the owner which recommendations to adopt, then update `org/goals.md` and log decisions.
