# Marcana 3PL

Source: the live website copy in `marcana-3pl-founder-update/llms.txt`. Keep the two in step.

## What it is
Third-party logistics and e-commerce fulfillment company in Miami, Florida. Warehousing,
pick-and-pack and same-day shipping for online brands on Shopify, Amazon, TikTok Shop,
WooCommerce and Etsy. Founder-led by Saul Ortiz. Bilingual: English and Spanish.
Companion to Vende en USA (venderenusa.com).

## Offer
- $149/month + $1.95 per order shipped (up to 2 lbs and 2 items). Carrier shipping billed at cost.
- 14-day free trial: no fulfillment fees, carrier cost only.
- Services: e-commerce fulfillment, TikTok Shop fulfillment, Amazon FBA prep, Amazon FBM,
  FedEx One Rate fulfillment, inventory management on ShipHero.
- Industries: apparel, vitamins and supplements, cosmetics, consumable goods.

## Customers
B2B: e-commerce brand owners who need a US fulfillment partner, including Spanish-speaking
importers selling into the United States.
