## What it is
Spanish-language service at venderenusa.com that helps foreign brand owners bring products into
the United States: FDA agent, first-import planning and coordination. Attention led by Saul Ortiz.
Group: services. Marcana 3PL is its fulfillment companion.

## Offer (from the website, 2026-11-15)
- "Tu Agente FDA": US$599 first year per facility, US$199/year renewal (auto-renew with express
  consent and a reminder 30 days before).
- "Tu Marca en EE. UU.": US$399 one-time, for someone about to pay a factory deposit (e.g. China).
- "Tu Primera Importación": US$499 one-time, from document review to delivery.
- Other filings (food, updates, coordination) quoted separately. Landed-cost calculator.
- Promise style: written scope and price before hiring, no mandatory sales call,
  "sin promesas de aprobación" (never promise FDA approval).
